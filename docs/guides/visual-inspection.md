# Images for design review

The AI can capture images directly from the model while working on your design.
Models with prepared views also offer a **Saved view** selector in the viewer.
The [Master Suite Gable House tour](master-suite-gable-house.md) illustrates twelve
views of a complete framed shell and its services. **Restore view** returns to the
selected view's settings; **Reset presentation** returns to the default uncut model.
Ask it to show a floor plan, look into a room, remove the roof from a view, or
reveal the framing or services in a wall. These views change what is displayed;
they do not remove construction from your design.

Useful review requests include:

- “Show the interior from above so I can check the bedroom and bathroom layout.”
- “Show this wall's framing and electrical boxes, with the new outlet highlighted.”
- “Look from the bedroom doorway toward the bathroom.”
- “Show the same view before and after moving the partition.”

Top-down plans use parallel projection, keeping the layout easy to compare.
Angled views help show height and depth. Section cuts remove the parts above or
beside a chosen plane and fill the exposed material faces so wall thicknesses and
openings remain visible. A view can also hide individual layers while retaining
framing or nearby construction.

The capture includes an image and a report identifying its design revision. When
numbers appear in an image, its component legend identifies the corresponding
parts. The AI can use those identities to inspect dimensions and make a focused
edit. A component can be concealed by another component even when both belong to
the view; request a different angle or cut when necessary.

Visual review complements the engine's dimensions, clearances and connection
checks. Ask the AI to investigate a suspected problem and verify the correction
in the same view. A successful image capture by itself is not a completed review.

The [interactive viewer](viewer-guide.md) provides related controls for exploring
the model yourself. Saved command views are separate from the viewer's current
camera and selection. The [AI visual-review reference](../../skills/home-design/references/visual-review.md)
describes command setup, view options and image interpretation.
